GFOX ORGANISATION
Tax & Financial Constitution

Official Regulatory Framework

Preamble

This constitution outlines the financial regulations, monthly dues, taxation policies, and contractual obligations for all official members of the GFOX ORGANISATION. By carrying the GFOX name, joining official groups, or accepting a salary from the GFOX ORGANISATION, members formally agree to adhere to the statutes detailed within these Acts.

Act I: Membership Dues and Branding

This act governs the financial obligations tied to representing the GFOX ORGANISATION and utilizing its community platforms.

Article 1

Name Tag Tax

To maintain the prestige, exclusivity, and branding of the GFOX ORGANISATION, a premium monthly fee is required to represent the team in-game.

  • Monthly Rate: 1,000 BDT
  • Condition: Any player actively wearing or using the "GFOX" tag in their username across any competitive or casual gaming platforms is subject to this tax.
Article 2

GFOX ORGANISATION Group Fee

Membership in the official communications group requires a monthly maintenance fee to support community management and administration.

  • Monthly Rate: 50 BDT
  • Condition: This applies to all members residing in the GFOX FAMILY 🧡 group chat or server.

Act II: Payroll and Revenue

This act outlines the taxation applied to members who are officially employed and salaried by the GFOX ORGANISATION.

Article 3

Income Tax

Players who receive an official salary through the GFOX ORGANISATION default payroll must contribute a percentage back to the treasury to fund management, growth, and operational costs.

  • Tax Rate: 30%
  • Condition: This deduction applies automatically to any standard salary or monetary compensation paid directly by the GFOX ORGANISATION to the player.

Act III: Contractual Stability and Resignation

This act dictates the rules regarding team stability, roster changes, and the penalties for breaking the standard notice period.

Article 4

Leave Notice & Buyout Clause

To ensure team stability and prevent sudden roster disruptions, the GFOX ORGANISATION strictly enforces a resignation notice period.

  • Standard Notice Period: Players must provide three (3) months of advanced written notice to management prior to leaving the team, exiting the group, or dropping the GFOX tag.
  • Immediate Leave Penalty (Buyout): If a player wishes to leave immediately without fulfilling the three-month notice period, they are required to pay an immediate release fee of 2,000 BDT.
  • Consequence of Default: Failure to pay this buyout fee upon an immediate exit will result in being officially blacklisted from the GFOX ORGANISATION and its affiliates.

Act IV: Administrative Procedures & Conduct

This act explains how financial obligations are fulfilled and establishes strict codes of conduct regarding the public image and allegiances of the GFOX ORGANISATION.

Article 5

Payment Terms and Collection

  • Billing Cycle: All standard monthly dues (Name Tag Tax and Group Fee totaling 1,050 BDT) must be paid in full by the 1st day of every calendar month.
  • Salaried Members: Management reserves the right to deduct the 1,050 BDT standard dues, alongside the 30% Income Tax, directly from a player's salary prior to payout.
  • Non-Salaried Members: Members not receiving a salary must manually send their dues to the designated GFOX ORGANISATION treasury accounts (e.g., bKash, Nagad, Rocket) by the deadline.
Article 6

Anti-Defamation and Public Image Clause

To protect the reputation and standing of the team, strict public relations rules apply to all current members.

  • Defamation Fine: 5,000 BDT
  • Condition: Players are strictly prohibited from saying anything bad, negative, disparaging, or defamatory about the GFOX ORGANISATION. Any player found violating this rule in public, in other group chats, or on social media will be instantly subjected to this fine.
Article 7

Rival Organization Fine

To ensure absolute loyalty among its members, the GFOX ORGANISATION strictly monitors support for rival factions.

  • Monthly Fine: 1,000 BDT
  • Condition: Any current member found to be actively supporting, cheering for, or promoting any other team except GFOX will be subjected to an ongoing penalty fee of 1,000 BDT for every month that this support continues.
